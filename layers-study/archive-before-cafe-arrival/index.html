The layers begin before the front door.

The earlier colours mostly separated activities. These studies instead show the changes you experience: open ground → covered threshold → shared interior → contained rooms. Nesting connects those settings within one compact building.

We do not build the whole plot. Each option uses a checked 408 m² outer envelope, about 27.4% of the measured plot, inside a 4 m inset. Around 1,080 m² remains outside the envelope. Ramp geometry and the complete room-area schedule still need to be resolved.

New boundary lines: heavy dark lines suggest walls; paired blue lines suggest glazing; dashed lines show cover overhead. Gaps show doorways or open passages. These clarify the bubbles, not a final wall layout or wall thickness.

Why this revision

The seven city conditions are evidence, not seven literal rings to reproduce. This translates the change between street, crossing, arrival and interior into a connected spatial sequence. Protection remains a design requirement, not a claim of measured comfort.

  • House N — architect-provided description: nesting makes a gradual relationship between street and interior. We borrow the relationship, not the whole-site enclosing shell.
  • Sala Beckett — Flores & Prats and Liquid Light: shared circulation has its own spatial character. This informs shared places, not our form.
Geometry and area audit — read before calling this a solution
  • Instructor plot: 02-project/drawings/_draw_data.json. Measured area 1,488.5 m²; nominal brief site 1,500 m². Smaller measured area used for the 30% test.
  • 17 × 24 m rectangle, rotated −17°, centre (0,5) in the source local frame. Minimum boundary clearance 4.41 m. Full audit.
  • 408 m² is a conservative outer projected envelope. Porch and upper overlaps are included; a court does not justify enlarging it. Open land is not automatically garden or public.
  • Ground porch: 17 × 3 m = 51 m². Four outer plates = 1,632 m²; less that porch = a crude 1,581 m² ceiling before further losses. B removes another 60 m² for a 3 × 5 m court over four levels. C reserves 40 m² total for floor openings. These are bounding checks, NOT usable areas or a compliant program schedule.
  • The 1,500 m² program includes 360 m² circulation/services/covered parking. B1 is not free additional area. B1 floor counting and porch/growing/terrace definitions need instructor clarification.
  • Sections show a schematic 14 m body within the 17 m cap; structure, roof equipment, headroom, exits and step-free paths still need design.
  • Street 80 and C1 indications are schematic. South arrival does not prove a legal or level connection from the stair landing. East ramp arrows are requirements only, not a solution for slope, turning, parking capacity or service separation.
