01 · Linked pockets

Nest spaces along a shared route.

Editable vector drawingPNG drawingBack to comparison

How it works

From the southern stairs, enter a welcome space. The eastern arrival meets it before the route unfolds through display, making and shared learning.

The shared route continues through overlapping landings. Smaller meeting rooms sit within the larger work layer; the lift reaches the same shared spaces as the public stair.

Next design test: Give each shared pocket a purpose: welcome, display, exchange.

02 · Shared court

Nest rooms around a common outdoor space.

Editable vector drawingPNG drawingBack to comparison

How it works

Both entrances reach the same welcome space. From here, a public loop opens onto a shared court; display, learning and cafe spaces address this centre.

Upper galleries overlook the open court. Shared circulation surrounds it, with quieter rooms behind. Growing occupies part of an upper edge, beside support.

Next design test: Keep the southern entrance visible; test daylight and floor area.

03 · Vertical commons

Nest shared places between the floors.

Editable vector drawingPNG drawingBack to comparison

How it works

The southern and eastern doors meet at a compact lobby. Cafe, display and a demonstration space animate the ground. A visible lift and stair lead toward shared upper places.

A stepped sequence of voids links the floors visually. Public galleries sit between learning and work layers. A terrace connects to growing at the upper level.

Next design test: Keep an active ground level and equal lift access to every landing.
