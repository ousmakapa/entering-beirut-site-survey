NestingThe sheltered heart

An asymmetrical, vertical nest

Screen evidence · corridor edge · Apr 2023

Start with the site, not the word. My concept is Nesting, but I did not choose it because I wanted a courtyard or boxes inside boxes. I chose it because this plot has no naturally protected side. The project must create the shelter that the site does not give it.
01 · Pressure

The plot has no sheltered end.

02 / 09

The plot is almost fully exposed to the highway condition. The direct highway face is short, but noise, wind and open sky affect the whole clearing. The north-west is also closed by a large industrial shed very close to the boundary. There is no neighbouring building that creates useful shade, and the nearby green is private. So the site does not contain a ready-made place of refuge.
02 · Release

The nest opens here.

When the edge map is read with access and views, one side behaves differently. The east faces the living fabric, carries the usable walkable frontage, gives the clearest public address and the widest open view. The bridge and bus stop do not land on the plot, so the building cannot depend on them. It must make the eastern arrival legible by itself.

Analysis → operation

The building makes the shelter.

EXPOSEDpressure reaches the clearing
THICKENhard uses take the hard edges
OPEN EASTthe heart becomes the address
LIFT THE HEARTentry → forum → work → garden
This is the conclusion from the analysis. First, the clearing is exposed. Second, robust program thickens the south, west and north-west. Third, the protective body is cut open toward the east, so the shared interior is public rather than hidden. Fourth, that heart rises through the section. Nesting is therefore not a shape placed on the site; it is the sequence produced by the site.
03 · Section

Not a courtyard.
A changing heart.

The void moves.
The frame stays.

05 / 09
A full-height courtyard would remove too much usable floor and would turn nesting into a formal hole. Instead, the heart changes at every level. It begins as an entrance cut, expands into a double-height public forum, becomes an occupied bridge or overlook at the work level, and finishes as an ecological room and terrace. The changing void gives nesting a sectional logic.

The program is not stacked randomly. The functions that tolerate noise, service and heavy use make the robust ground and outer layers. Exhibition, café and community uses form the visible public heart. Quieter work and learning sit above that activity. The ecological program crowns the sequence, giving the public route a destination and creating the upper sheltered layer.
05 · Movement

One route reveals every layer.

East forecourt → loggia → forum → stair → garden

07 / 09
The nest must be understandable from the eastern approach. A planted widening leads to a shaded loggia. The lobby does not end the route; it expands into exhibition and the public forum. A visible stair continues upward through community and work overlooks to the planted top. Cars, delivery and service remain separate at the south-east and hard edges, so public movement does not conflict with them.

Nesting is also environmental. The south becomes deep and robust against the corridor. The north-west accepts that it is a back and holds core, storage and technical space. The west is filtered with depth and vertical shading. The east opens through shaded loggias rather than unprotected glass. Fresh air is taken from controlled east or roof positions because the useful wind also crosses the corridor; I am not treating wind and clean air as the same thing.
09 / 09
Selected concept

Nesting

Because the plot has no sheltered end, robust functions form protective outer layers, a public heart opens east, and the shared space rises into an ecological upper nest.

Protect → open → gather → rise

Close with one sentence. Because the plot is exposed to infrastructure and closed by industrial edges, the project creates the shelter that the site lacks. Robust functions form the outer layers, public and communal life occupies a changing inner heart, and the nest opens toward the eastern arrival before rising into an ecological upper layer.
